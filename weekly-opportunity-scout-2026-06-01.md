## Weekly Synthesis

### Key Themes This Week

1. **AI Governance legislation is globalizing fast.** Multiple governments (Malaysia, Florida, Ontario, Ireland) have active RFPs for AI governance framework consultants. The legislative-drafting angle is maturing — these aren't study contracts, they're build-the-law engagements.

2. **Big philanthropy is deploying capital at scale.** The Humanity AI coalition ($500M pooled fund from Ford, MacArthur, Mozilla, Omidyar, Mellon, and others) is moving from announcement to grantmaking in 2026. Pooled-fund grants are beginning to flow across democracy, education, labor, culture, and security. This is the single largest coordinated philanthropic bet on public-interest AI.

3. **AI safety fellowships have proliferated.** Anthropic, OpenAI, CAIS, IAPS, CBAI, and CIP all have active or recently-closed fellowship programs. The OpenAI Safety Fellowship (Sept 2026–Feb 2027) is still accepting applications. The field is actively seeking practitioners, not just academics.

4. **Digital public goods and DPI remain a growth area.** The DPGA is hiring for multiple remote contract roles, the World Bank's Global DPI Program is scaling, and Rockefeller Foundation's AI Readiness Project is expanding to all 50 US states. There's sustained demand for senior technical advisors who understand both the infrastructure and the governance.

5. **Civic tech organizations are investing in digital sovereignty.** CIVICUS has multiple concurrent consultancies (research, senior advisory, advocacy) under its Digital Democracy Initiative, all fully remote and explicitly targeting senior experts.

### Notable Shifts

- The Coefficient Giving / Open Philanthropy split means AI governance and safety RFPs are now issued under the Coefficient Giving brand. Their AI Governance RFP closed in January 2026 but they've signaled more are coming. The Technical AI Safety RFP ($40M budget) had an extended deadline that has also passed, but late submissions may still be considered.
- Government procurement of AI governance consulting is accelerating at the state/provincial level (Florida, Ontario), not just national level.
- Mozilla's Democracy x AI Cohort represents a new model: incubator-style grants ($50K + potential $250K sustain track) rather than traditional project funding.

### Gaps Where Proactive Pitching May Work

- **World Bank WDR 2026 ("Decoding AI for Development")**: The flagship report is in production. Contributing author or peer reviewer roles may be available through direct outreach to the research team.
- **Rockefeller Foundation AI Readiness Project**: The pilot program across US states will need technical consultants for responsible AI deployment. No public RFP yet — cold outreach to Center for Civic Futures is warranted.
- **OECD AI Technology Action Plan 2026–2030**: Multi-stakeholder consultation and sectoral working groups are forming. Expert participation may be available through direct engagement with NAIO.
- **Humanity AI pooled fund**: Grant application details are still being finalized for 2026. Signing up at humanityai.ai for early notification and establishing relationships with the managing entity (Rockefeller Philanthropy Advisors) would be strategic.

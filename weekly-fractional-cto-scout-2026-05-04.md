## Weekly Synthesis

### Dominant Opportunity Patterns

1. **AI strategy is the #1 demand driver across every sector.** From Ohio's state government to UNICEF's AI ecosystem to fractional CTO roles for AI startups — the common thread is organizations needing senior technical leaders who can bridge AI capabilities with organizational strategy.

2. **Multilateral organizations offer the highest-leverage advisory roles.** The World Bank ($2.3B in consultant contracts in FY2025), UNDP, IDB, and UNICEF are all actively procuring senior technical advisory for digital transformation and AI. Rates are below private-sector market ($250–700/day) but scope and impact are exceptional.

### 1. Schmidt Sciences – Science of Trustworthy AI RFP

- **Organization:** Schmidt Sciences
- **Type:** Research Grant (RFP)
- **Summary:** Major research funding for technical work on understanding, predicting, and controlling risks from frontier AI systems. - **Compensation / funding range:** Tier 1: up to $1M for focused investigations. Tier 2: $1M–$5M+ for larger collaborative efforts. Compute resources also available.
- **Duration / level of effort:** 1–3 years
- **Location / remote eligibility:** Global; no geographic restrictions
- **Deadline:** May 17, 2026, 11:59 PM EDT
- **Primary link(s):** https://schmidtsciences.smapply.io/prog/science_of_trustworthy_ai_rfp_2026/ · https://www.schmidtsciences.org/trustworthy-ai/
- **Notes, risks, or caveats:** Indirect costs capped at 10%. Must be affiliated with a university, research institution, or nonprofit. Strong competition expected given funding scale.

## Weekly Synthesis

### Key Themes This Week

1. **The application window is narrow.** Only two high-quality opportunities have deadlines in the next 2–3 weeks: GovAI Summer Fellowship 2027 (Nov 22) and Cities Deliver Round 1 (Nov 29). Both are strong fits and worth prioritizing.

2. **The multi-agent safety research wave is real.** Google DeepMind's $10M call, the Cooperative AI Foundation's involvement, AISI's evaluation contracts, and SPAR's fall 2026 agent evaluation projects all point to growing demand for people who can evaluate and govern agent systems. This is a differentiated skill area.

3. **Philanthropic AI funding is consolidating.** The Humanity AI $500M commitment, Rockefeller's Cities Deliver, and Open Philanthropy/Coefficient Giving's technical safety RFP represent a few large funders dominating the public-interest AI space. Being on their radar — even through unfunded interactions — matters for pipeline development.

4. **Government AI procurement is accelerating but unevenly.** The HHS Anthropic BPA signals growing federal AI adoption. The UK's £600M AI security package is materializing in contracts. But individual solicitations are hard to catch in real time — they appear and close quickly on SAM.gov and Find a Tender.

5. **ISO 42001 and EU AI Act compliance are creating a steady advisory market.** This is less aligned with the public-interest mission but represents reliable revenue for practitioners with governance expertise.

## Methodology & Limitations

This scan searched across five source categories in parallel: multilateral/international organizations, national/subnational government procurement, foundations/NGOs/mission-driven orgs, enterprises/startups/VCs, and fractional networks/community signals. Approximately 90 distinct web searches were executed.

**Critical limitation:** WebFetch was blocked by the session's network egress proxy for every external domain attempted (including procurement portals, job boards, and even control domains). No page could be opened directly. All items below are derived from search-result titles, URLs, and snippet text. Every deadline, rate, and scope detail should be re-confirmed at source before acting. Where a detail did not appear verbatim in search results, "not stated" is written rather than estimated. No opportunity, URL, or reference number below is invented.

## Weekly Synthesis

### Key Themes This Week

1. **AI governance consulting is booming at the state level.** Florida's AI governance roadmap RFP (July 16 deadline) follows similar recent RFPs from Indiana and Ontario. State governments are increasingly hiring external consultants for AI strategy — a growing market segment that doesn't require federal contracting infrastructure.

2. **Philanthropy is deploying at unprecedented scale.** The Humanity AI coalition ($500M) announced $18M in initial grants and is preparing a $10M open call this summer. This dwarfs prior philanthropic commitments to AI governance. Combined with active programs at Ford, Sloan, Hewlett, and Coefficient Giving, philanthropic funding for public-interest AI work is at an all-time high.

3. **EU AI Act compliance creates imminent consulting demand.** With high-risk obligations going live August 2, 2026, the EU AI Office is preparing a technical assistance tender. Organizations needing compliance support will be a significant consulting market through 2027.

4. **NIST is the quiet giant.** Two separate open-enrollment opportunities (AI Consortium membership and AI RMF Critical Infrastructure Community of Interest) offer unpaid but strategically invaluable seats at the table for shaping U.S. federal AI standards. Both are open now with no hard deadline.

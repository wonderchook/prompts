## Weekly Synthesis

### Key Themes This Week

**1. The Coefficient Giving / Humanity AI axis is now the center of gravity for public-interest AI funding.** Coefficient Giving's imminent Global Health + AI RFP (Aug 21 deadline) and Humanity AI's forthcoming $10M open call represent the two most significant new funding channels for senior practitioners in this space. Both are explicitly designed for the kind of cross-sector, governance-aware, development-oriented AI work the target profile excels at.

**2. UN agency AI procurement is accelerating, but unevenly.** UNDP has active AI governance consultancies in Montenegro, Bangladesh, Laos, and Ukraine – all small (20–30 day) contracts, but they signal a systematic capacity gap across country offices. The pattern suggests opportunity for a proactive pitch to UNDP's Digital, AI and Innovation Hub rather than chasing individual procurements.

**3. The US AI safety fellowship ecosystem is maturing rapidly.** FAS, OpenAI, Anthropic, RAND CAST, Horizon Institute, and CAIS now offer overlapping but differentiated fellowship tracks. The FAS fellowship (deadline Sep 7) stands out for its part-time, policy-entrepreneurship model – it's designed to be stacked with other work, not to absorb all attention.

**4. EU AI Act compliance is generating consulting demand but not through the usual channels.** August 2, 2026 marked full enforcement of high-risk system obligations. The consulting market is dominated by law firms and Big Four, but the governance-design and evaluation work sits upstream of compliance and is less well served. This is a proactive-pitch opportunity, not an RFP to respond to.

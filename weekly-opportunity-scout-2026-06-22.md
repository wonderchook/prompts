## Weekly Synthesis

### Key Themes This Week

1. **Philanthropic convergence on public-interest AI is accelerating.** The Humanity AI consortium ($500M from 10 foundations) represents an unprecedented pooling of philanthropic capital around AI and the public interest. The $10M open call expected this summer should be treated as the marquee opportunity to watch.

2. **AI governance fellowships remain the richest pipeline.** GovAI (July 12 deadline), Anthropic (rolling), and RAND CAST (rolling quarterly) all offer structured pathways with meaningful compensation. The GovAI winter fellowship deadline is 20 days away.

3. **Government AI strategy consulting is a growing market.** State-level RFPs (Florida AI roadmap, July 16) and federal programs (NSF TechAccess at $168M+) signal expanding demand for AI governance consultants in the public sector. Canada's pre-qualified AI supplier list is a strategic asset worth pursuing.

4. **The UK is the most active government funder of AI safety research.** AISI's Alignment Project (£27M+ in Round 1, Round 2 imminent), Systemic Safety Grants, 8 open staff positions, and the Alan Turing Open Source AI Fellowship collectively represent the deepest government commitment to public-interest AI safety.

5. **International organizations are actively procuring AI governance expertise.** UNDP has multiple active consultant notices (Montenegro, Laos, India). UNESCO is building out its AI Ethics unit with several consultancy positions. The ITU/GovStack roster system provides a systematic entry point.

6. **Civic tech organizations are in a hiring cycle.** CIVICUS posted multiple Digital Democracy Initiative consultancies in May. While deadlines have passed, direct outreach is warranted. The DPI Safeguards Accelerator (UNDP) has an August 15 deadline for country proposals that embed advisory roles.

### Gaps Where Proactive Pitching May Work

- **Coefficient Giving / Open Philanthropy:** They've shifted from open RFPs to proactive identification. Direct outreach to program officers is now the primary path.
- **Ford Foundation, Rockefeller, Hewlett, Packard:** None accept unsolicited proposals. Relationship-building with program officers is essential. Humanity AI is the best current pathway to Ford-adjacent funding.
- **CIVICUS Digital Democracy Initiative:** Multiple roles recently closed. Organization is clearly building capacity — a speculative inquiry may land well.
- **DPGA / Co-Develop Fund grantee network:** Co-Develop's grantees (MOSIP, OpenCRVS, OpenFn, Mojaloop) frequently need senior consultants for implementation.

### Emerging Patterns

- **Philanthropic funders are now outpacing the federal government in AI ethics/governance funding.** This shift creates significant advisory demand.
- **"Systemic" and "sociotechnical" safety framings are gaining institutional traction** (UK AISI Systemic Safety Grants, Humanity AI's framing around "communities closest to AI's impact").
- **The DPI/digital public goods ecosystem continues to professionalize**, with UNDP, UNICEF, GovStack, and Co-Develop all expanding their consultant rosters.

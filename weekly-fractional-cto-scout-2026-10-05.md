## Executive Summary

This week's scan surfaces a mix of multilateral procurement, government modernization, venture studio co-founding, and fractional CTO engagements. The standout opportunity is the **Digital Public Goods Alliance consultancy** with an application deadline of today (October 5) — act immediately if interested. The IDB and ADB continue to be strong sources for senior digital transformation advisory work, and two venture studios are actively seeking technical co-founders with strategic scope. Government modernization procurement in the US (Georgia) and UK (GDS Local) continues to expand, though most require firm-level bids rather than individual consulting.

**Key pattern this week:** AI governance and readiness assessment demand is accelerating across multilateral organizations. The OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026 confirms that only 28% of countries systematically evaluate AI use in government — signaling sustained consulting demand for senior practitioners who can bridge strategy and implementation.

## Weekly Synthesis

### Dominant Patterns

1. **AI governance and readiness is the dominant demand signal.** The OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026 confirms massive gaps in government AI evaluation capabilities. The IDB, ADB, and World Bank are all hiring for AI + digital transformation advisory roles. This is a multi-year demand cycle, not a trend.

2. **Multilateral procurement remains the richest vein for high-trust advisory work.** The IDB, ADB, World Bank, and UNICEF all have open positions combining strategic scope with senior stakeholder access. These organizations value judgment and institutional navigation — exactly the profile of a fractional CTO with 15+ years experience.

3. **Venture studios are actively recruiting technical co-founders**, particularly for B2B AI ventures. These are equity-based, not consulting, but the strategic scope and autonomy align perfectly with the target profile.

4. **Government procurement (US, UK) is expanding but requires firm-level participation.** Georgia's Cloud-First RFQC and the UK's GDS Local initiative represent large-scale modernization budgets, but individual consultants typically need to engage through a prime contractor or establish a consulting entity.

### Shifts vs. Previous Periods

- **USDS/18F ecosystem has contracted.** 18F was dissolved in March 2025, and USDS was reorganized under DOGE. The US federal civic tech landscape is significantly reduced compared to 2023–2024. State-level government (e.g., Georgia) is now the more active procurement channel.
- **DPGA and digital public goods ecosystem growing.** Canada's CDS joined DPGA in June 2026, expanding the institutional base. More procurement tooling and implementation work is being funded.
- **Fractional CTO market rates stabilizing.** The $10K–$15K/month range at 15 hours/week appears well-established. Premium advisory work (board sessions, due diligence) commands $2,500–$5,000/day.

### Gaps — Where Proactive Outreach May Be Effective

1. **National AI readiness assessments.** Only 28% of OECD countries evaluate AI use in government. Proactively pitching AI readiness assessment services to digital ministries in mid-income countries (where World Bank/ADB funding is available) could be highly effective.

2. **DPGA downstream implementation.** The procurement tooling consultancy (Tier 1) is a wedge into the broader DPG implementation ecosystem. Even if the deadline has passed, building a relationship with DPGA for future engagements is worthwhile.

3. **Technical due diligence for AI-native companies.** The market for AI due diligence has expanded significantly (AKF Partners, Deloitte, boutiques). Independent consultants with architecture and AI expertise can compete in this space, particularly for PE/VC deals in the $10M–$100M range.

4. **UK GDS Local.** The new GDS Local initiative is actively seeking technology partners for local government collaboration. Early engagement could position for consulting work as the program scales.

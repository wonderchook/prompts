## Weekly Synthesis

### Key Themes This Week

1. **Philanthropic AI funding is accelerating and consolidating.** Humanity AI ($500M), Coefficient Giving (rolling $40M+ for safety alone), and the OpenAI Foundation ($50M) represent a massive pipeline. The smart move is relationship-building with program officers now, before the next open calls drop.

2. **AI governance consulting is a growing market with real RFPs.** Malaysia's AI Governance Bill, the OECD Policy Observatory, and Rockefeller's AI Readiness Project all represent paid advisory work at the national/international level. This is no longer just academic — governments are buying expertise.

3. **Open-source AI governance is becoming institutionalized.** The OSI fellowship at Duke, Coefficient's open-source alignment work, and the Digital Public Goods Alliance all signal that "open-source AI" is transitioning from a community norm to a policy domain requiring dedicated researchers and advisors.

4. **Agent systems and evaluation are the hot technical frontier.** USSOCOM's agentic AI RFI, Brookings' work on evaluating agentic AI, and the DAI 2026 conference call all point to growing demand for expertise in agent systems governance — a niche that combines technical depth with policy relevance.

### Gaps Where Proactive Pitching May Work

- **Rockefeller/Center for Civic Futures AI Readiness Project**: No public RFP, but they are actively building out state-level pilots and a knowledge hub. A cold pitch offering technical architecture for the knowledge hub or evaluation frameworks for pilots could land.

- **Humanity AI coalition members individually**: While the pooled fund's open call is pending, individual members (Ford Technology & Society, Mozilla Foundation, Omidyar Network) have their own grantmaking. Pitch directly to program officers with a specific proposal bridging two pillars (e.g., "AI agent systems in democratic participation").

- **OSI Open Source AI Fellowship**: The founding fellow is in place, but the program is new and industry-backed. Pitch an advisory or mentorship role to OSI leadership, or propose a complementary research workstream.

- **UK AI Security Institute (AISI)**: Limited public contracting visible, but the Institute is growing rapidly and actively commissioning external research. Submit an unsolicited proposal through Find a Tender (UK procurement portal) or approach via academic partners at Oxford or Turing.

### Notable Patterns

- **Repeat funders**: Coefficient Giving / Open Philanthropy appears across multiple tiers (safety, governance, global health). They are the single most active funder in this space and worth cultivating as a primary relationship.
- **Emerging funder**: Humanity AI is new but enormous. Their governance structure (Rockefeller Philanthropy Advisors as fund manager) means they'll need external expertise to evaluate proposals and design programs.
- **Deadline cluster**: GovAI Research Scholar (Aug 16) and Malaysia public consultation (Jul 31) are both actionable this week.

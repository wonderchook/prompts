# Weekly Opportunity Scout Report — 2026-05-18
_Public-Interest AI, Open Systems, & Socio-Technical Leadership_

## Weekly Synthesis

### Key Themes This Week

1. **Philanthropy is consolidating around public-interest AI.** The Humanity AI coalition (Ford, MacArthur, Omidyar, Mozilla, et al.) announced $18M in new grants on May 12 and is preparing a **$10M open call this summer**. This is the clearest signal yet that major foundations are moving from exploratory grants to sustained, large-scale AI-governance funding. Anyone positioned at the intersection of AI systems and public policy should be tracking this closely.

2. **AI safety research funding is at a high-water mark.** Schmidt Sciences ($1M–$5M+ grants, deadline just passed May 17), Open Philanthropy ($40M committed), Anthropic, MATS, and CAIS are all running active programs. The field has enough money that the bottleneck is credible researchers, not capital — which favors senior practitioners who can frame novel research agendas.

3. **Government AI readiness is creating advisory demand.** The Rockefeller Foundation's AI Readiness Project is scaling to all 50 U.S. states, the UK's AI Assurance Innovation Fund opens in spring 2026, and the EU AI Act reaches full applicability in August 2026. Each of these creates consulting demand for people who can translate between policy frameworks and technical implementation.

4. **Digital public goods infrastructure is professionalizing.** DPGA is hiring for its secretariat, UNESCO is aligning its open solutions to the DPG roadmap, and the World Bank's WDR 2026 is focused entirely on AI for development. The niche of "open systems + international development" is becoming a mainstream funding category.

### Gaps Where Proactive Pitching May Work

- **State-level AI governance consulting (U.S.):** The Rockefeller/CCF AI Readiness Project is funding pilots in 50 states but needs technical advisors. No public RFP — this is a cold-outreach opportunity to CCF directly.
- **EU AI Act compliance advisory:** Full applicability is August 2026. European companies and public-sector bodies will need independent advisory on high-risk AI system classification, conformity assessment, and transparency obligations. The consultation on transparency guidelines closes June 3.
- **Humanity AI open call pre-positioning:** The $10M open call hasn't launched yet. Now is the time to build relationships with the program team and shape a proposal concept before the call drops.

### Notable Shifts vs. Previous Weeks

- USAID's dismantling has scattered thousands of development professionals into the market, compressing rates for generalist development consulting but **increasing demand for specialized AI/data governance expertise** that incumbents don't have.
- The UN Scientific Panel on AI (40 members, appointed Feb 2026) is now operational, which will generate downstream demand for technical briefings, evidence submissions, and commissioned research.

## Weekly Synthesis

### Key Themes This Week

1. **Philanthropic AI governance funding is maturing.** Humanity AI's $10M open call represents a new scale of coordinated foundation investment in public-interest AI. Ten foundations pooling money into a single vehicle signals the field has moved past experimental grants into sustained infrastructure funding. This is the biggest single opening this week.

2. **AI auditing is becoming an institution.** AVERI's launch, active hiring, and pilot audit program reflect a broader shift: third-party AI auditing is moving from concept to practice. The International AI Safety Report 2026, the NIST AI RMF, and ISO 42001 are all creating demand for people who can bridge technical evaluation and governance frameworks.

3. **Digital public goods procurement is a growing niche.** The DPGA consultancy and the World Bank's DPI Procurement Guide both point to a gap: governments want to adopt open technologies but lack practical procurement tools. This is an underserved area where engineering leadership + open-source experience is directly applicable.

4. **Humanitarian and development orgs are building internal AI capacity.** NetHope's fellowship, UNDP's digital strategy, and the ITU-UNDP responsible AI governance course all signal that international development organizations are moving past "should we use AI?" to "how do we govern it?" They need experienced practitioners, not just researchers.

### Gaps Where Proactive Pitching May Work

- **AI agent governance consulting.** The CSA, DoD, and Forrester reports all document a massive governance gap around agentic AI systems. No major RFP specifically targets this yet, but organizations (government, enterprise, humanitarian) are struggling with agent identity, authorization, and accountability. A targeted pitch to NIST, an AI safety lab, or a foundation could create an engagement.

- **Open-source AI policy advisory.** The OSI fellowship isn't open yet, and the OECD is between formal consultant calls. A pre-positioned pitch offering open-source AI governance advisory (leveraging the EU AI Act compliance wave and the open-source AI definition debate) could land with any of these organizations.

- **World Bank country-level AI governance.** The WDR 2026 and the FY26–30 Digital & AI Strategy create a mandate for country offices to implement AI governance frameworks. The Bank's consulting procurement cycle means opportunities will surface on the Development Business portal over the coming months.
